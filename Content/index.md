Hey, I'm **Amit Samant** — a Senior iOS Engineer at [Rakuten Viki](https://rakuten.viki.com) based in Singapore.

I've been building iOS apps and Swift libraries since 2018, with a focus on clean architecture, pixel-perfect UI, and tools that make other developers' lives easier. In 2020 I was selected as one of 350 global winners of Apple's WWDC Swift Student Challenge.

These days I'm exploring everything from local LLM inference on-device with llama.cpp, to cross-platform Swift via [Skip.tools](https://skip.tools), to fractals born from π's irrationality. When I'm not shipping code, I'm usually diving deep into Metal, CRDTs, or figuring out why `async/await` does what it does.
