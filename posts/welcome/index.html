Welcome to My Blog

I've been meaning to start writing for a while. A quick note on why I finally did — and what you can expect here.

Why Now

I spend a lot of time reading other people's posts about Swift and iOS development. Exploring corners of the language, digging into framework internals, building small tools to scratch itches. Most of that exploration lives in private notes or half-finished READMEs. A blog forces me to finish the thought.

What I'll Write About

Mostly the things I'm working on or thinking about:

  • Swift internals — language evolution, concurrency, the compiler
  • iOS architecture — patterns that hold up at scale vs. patterns that look good in tutorials
  • Developer tooling — building tools for other developers (like DeeplinkScout)
  • Experiments — cross-platform Swift via Skip, local LLMs on-device, Metal, CRDTs

I won't stick to any rigid schedule. I'll write when I have something worth saying.
